# Guide

## Order lifecycle

An order starts as open, moves to shipped once the warehouse picks it, and ends as delivered or cancelled. Customers can cancel until it ships.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within a week. Partial refunds are possible for single items.

## Notifications

Customers get an email when an order ships and another when it is delivered. Support staff are notified of cancellations.

## Customer accounts

Each customer has one account. Addresses and payment methods are stored on the account and reused across orders.

## Pricing and tax

Prices are shown tax-exclusive. Tax is added at checkout based on the delivery address.

## Glossary

Order: a request to buy one or more items. Item: a single product line. Fulfilment: picking, packing and shipping an order.
